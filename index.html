Privacy Policy — SFB Studio

Last updated: October 10, 2026

SFB Studio is a drum machine and step-sequencer app. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain language.

What the app collects

Nothing. SFB Studio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Anything you create in the app — your drum patterns, tempo settings, and downloaded loops — stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Internet use

The app works fully offline. It may load font files from Google Fonts when you are online so text renders nicely; no personal data is sent in that process.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
